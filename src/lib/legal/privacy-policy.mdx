## 1. **Introduction**

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you interact with our services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in a transparent and responsible manner.

---

## 2. **Information Collection**

We collect information you provide directly to us, as well as data generated from your interactions with our services. This may include personal identifiers, account credentials, payment information, communication data, and usage information. We collect this information through various channels, including website forms, email correspondence, and automated data collection technologies.

---

## 3. **Location and Network Information**

When you use features of our services that require location verification, such as capturing and submitting photos, we collect the following information:

- **Device location:** With your permission, we use your device's Location Services to collect your precise GPS coordinates, including latitude, longitude, and location accuracy, and, where your device provides them, altitude, heading, and speed. We collect this information while a capture session is active, including when the session begins, with each photo you take, and when the session ends. Precise location access is required to use these features.
- **Street address:** When a capture session begins, we send your GPS coordinates to Mapbox, a third-party mapping provider, which converts them into a street address, such as the street, neighborhood, city, region, postal code, and country of your location.
- **Network information:** When a capture session begins, we send your IP address to IPinfo, a third-party IP data provider, to determine your internet service provider, including its name, domain, and autonomous system number (ASN), as well as the country and continent associated with your network.

We store this information with your submission. You can deny or withdraw location access at any time through your device or browser settings; however, you will not be able to use features that require location verification while location access is turned off.

---

## 4. **Use of Information**

We use the collected information for several purposes, including but not limited to: providing and maintaining our services, improving and personalizing user experience, processing transactions, communicating about our services and offerings, conducting analytics and research, and ensuring the security and integrity of our platform. We use location and network information to verify where and when a submission was made, to confirm the authenticity of submissions (including by comparing the country of your network against the country of your device location), and to help detect and prevent fraud and misuse. We process your information in compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

---

## 5. **Data Sharing and Disclosure**

We may share your information in specific circumstances, including: with your explicit consent, to comply with legal obligations, to protect our rights and safety, with service providers who assist us in operating our business (including Mapbox, which receives your GPS coordinates to determine your street address, and IPinfo, which receives your IP address to determine your internet service provider and network location), and in connection with a business transfer or similar transaction. We implement appropriate safeguards when transferring data and ensure that third parties adhere to our privacy standards.

## 6. **Data Security**

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. These include encryption of data in transit and at rest, regular security assessments, access controls, and employee training on data protection. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 7. **Your Rights and Choices**

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, as well as the right to data portability and to withdraw consent. We are committed to facilitating the exercise of these rights and provide mechanisms for you to do so.

## 8. **International Data Transfers**

Your information may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 9. **Children's Privacy**

Our services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected or received personal information from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will delete that information.
